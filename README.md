# NEXUS TRADER

**NEXUS / TRADER → QUALIFY → PAPER TRADE → MEASURE**

NEXUS Trader is a desktop-focused stock analysis and paper-trading platform designed to turn market data into a repeatable trading workflow.

The objective is simple:

> **Find opportunities. Qualify them. Test them. Measure the results.**

NEXUS combines market scanning, technical analysis, trade qualification, paper trading, portfolio tracking, and performance measurement into a single interface.

# Core Features

### Market Scanner

Scan the market for stocks that meet configured trading and technical criteria.

### Ticker Analysis

Run deeper analysis against an individual symbol and generate a qualified trading candidate.

### Trade Qualification

Potential trades must pass NEXUS qualification rules before being promoted to actionable candidates.

### Paper Trading

Test strategies using simulated positions before committing real capital.

### Portfolio Tracking

Monitor open positions, account value, buying power, profit/loss, and trading performance.

### Strategy Measurement

NEXUS is built around measuring results rather than trusting signals blindly.

### Market Data Feeds

NEXUS can integrate external market-data providers such as Alpaca to obtain current and historical market information.

### Local Desktop Interface

Designed to operate as a Windows desktop application with the trading dashboard running locally.

# NEXUS Philosophy

Most trading systems stop at:

```text
SCAN → SIGNAL → BUY
```

NEXUS is designed around a longer feedback loop:

```text
DISCOVER
   ↓
ANALYZE
   ↓
QUALIFY
   ↓
PAPER TRADE
   ↓
MEASURE
   ↓
LEARN
   ↓
REFINE
   ↺
```

A trading strategy should prove itself through measurable results.

---

# Risk Management

Risk management is a core part of the qualification process.

A potential trade should consider:

```text
Entry
Stop Loss
Profit Target
Position Size
Risk / Reward
Available Capital
Strategy Confidence
```

A strong signal without acceptable risk is not necessarily a strong trade.
